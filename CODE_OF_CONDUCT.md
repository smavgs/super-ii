# Community Code of Conduct

Super ii welcomes people of different backgrounds, experience levels, and
viewpoints. Participate with respect, patience, honesty, and care.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, clear evidence,
respect for privacy and intellectual property, and feedback directed at the
work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, and attempts to
pressure contributors into unsafe actions are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect people or the project. Serious or
sensitive conduct concerns should be sent through the
[private contact form](https://superii.site/contact?interest=security), not
debated in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where
appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or unrelated personal information.
